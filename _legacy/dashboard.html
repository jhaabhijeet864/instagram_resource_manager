Analytics Precision: Understanding The Funnel Distinctions

A Download button click means someone clicked (intent). A Successful server response means your server or Google Drive served the file (delivery). Neither proves that the person opened or read the PDF (reader completion). This dashboard distinguishes all three separately so your analytics never become fiction.
